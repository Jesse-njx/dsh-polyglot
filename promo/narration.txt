dsh-polyglot — promo narration (~1 minute, 8 slides)
=====================================================
Read this aloud while screen-recording. Each block starts on its slide; hold
each slide ~7-8 seconds. Advance with the space bar or right arrow. Total run
time ≈ 60 seconds.

[Slide 1 — hero]
Meet dsh-polyglot: the model switch for DeepSeek Harness. One plugin that
points DSH at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — free ones, cheap ones, even
your own.

[Slide 2 — one plugin, two layers]
One plugin, two layers. A generic adapter that speaks to any OpenAI-compatible
API. And a router that chains your providers together.

[Slide 3 — the pain]
Sound familiar? Free tiers rate-limit constantly. Official prices keep
climbing. And everybody asks the same thing — can I use my own endpoint?

[Slide 4 — automatic failover]
So dsh-polyglot fails over automatically. A provider hits 429? It cools down,
and the next one picks the request up. Mid-task, no drama.

[Slide 5 — watch the chain]
Watch it happen: nous-portal slams into the free ceiling, and opencode-zen
takes over instantly. Then DeepSeek official. Then kilo. The chain keeps you
moving.

[Slide 6 — commands]
Switch chains mid-session with /model. And check exactly what you spent with
/polyglot usage — tokens and cost, straight from the session log.

[Slide 7 — presets]
Free and cheap DeepSeek, everywhere. Curated presets, one config, and a
default chain that lets you code all day for free.

[Slide 8 — install]
Install it in one command: dsh plugin, profile web, add,
at-dsh-polyglot-slash-bundle. Code all day for free.
